Open Science

Open, reliable and reproducible research

I believe science is stronger when it is transparent and reusable. I work to make data and code openly available, contribute to peer review and editorial service, and take part in community efforts to improve reproducibility in ecology and evolution.

Open data & code

Data and analysis code accompanying my papers are deposited in public repositories and linked from each article, so results can be checked and reused.

Reproducibility

I contribute to testing and improving data- and code-archiving standards across the British Ecological Society journals.

Community

Member of SORTEE and of BES Special Interest Groups, working towards open, reliable and transparent ecology and evolutionary biology.
